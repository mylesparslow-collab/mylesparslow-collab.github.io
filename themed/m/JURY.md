# Mockup M: "Redline" (jury sheet)

Written 2026-11-21 before the build. Shared rules: `themed/v4/BRIEF.md`.

**Concept sentence.** A portfolio laid out like a draft in review: the layout is one document page with a margin of comment balloons; the body is set in a legal document's serif, with comments and controls in a sans; the colour comes from the markup (what Myles did and what the AI did, each in its own colour); the motion behaves like Track Changes: markup is switched on and off, never animated for show.

**Why it is his.** He reviews legal documents for a living, built Stemma (version control and lost-edit recovery for legal drafts), and every write-up on the site already separates what he did from what the AI did ("I supplied the domain… Claude built the search engine…"). Redline makes that split visible.

**Tier / family.** Tier C: DOM and CSS only. No canvas, no WebGL. Family: own (redlined draft), nearest 01 editorial.

**References and moves.** (1) A real redline / compare document from a law firm: numbered articles and sections, insertions underlined in the reviser's colour, comment balloons in the right margin joined to their anchor by a thin leader, a "Simple markup / All markup / No markup / Original" view switch. Structure only: no Word ribbon, no blue, no fake toolbar icons. (2) Contract numbering: rooms are Articles 1–5, projects are Sections (1.1 … 5.20), exactly the project codes.

**Signature.** The markup switch. Every project's "How it was built" note is split into sentences and each sentence is attributed honestly by its grammatical subject: sentences whose subject is "I" (Myles) are Myles's; sentences whose subject is Claude, ChatGPT, Codex, Gemini, Suno or a local model are that tool's; anything else is neutral. In "All markup" the sentences carry their author's underline colour and the note appears as a comment balloon in the margin beside the project; in "No markup" the page is a clean document; a reviewer filter ("Show changes by: Myles · Claude · ChatGPT · Codex · Gemini · Suno · local models") dims every sentence and project that the chosen author did not touch. The homepage index also gets a small author bar per project (Myles and AI share, from the same split). Every attribution is computed from his own words; label it on the page as "attributed from each project's How it was built note".

**Type.** Document: Tinos (OFL, Times-metric, `~/Desktop/ClaudeStuff/fonts/google-fonts/ofl/tinos`), 400 and 700, 18–19 px body. Interface, balloons, review bar, labels: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (OFL, variable), 400/600, 13–15 px. Headings in Tinos 700 at clamp(28px, 3vw, 44px) for articles and clamp(40px, 5.5vw, 84px) for the title. Sentence case everywhere.

**Palette.** Desk `#e7e7e3`, page `#ffffff`, text `#141414`, dim `#5c5c58`, hairline `#d6d6d1`. Author colours (underlines, balloon edges, filter chips): Myles `#141414` (solid black double underline), Claude `#c8501a`, ChatGPT `#0e7c5e`, Codex `#7a6a00`, Gemini `#b0206e`, Suno `#9a5b00`, local models `#4a4a46`. Dark theme: desk `#111110`, page `#1c1c1a`, text `#ecebe6`, author colours lightened to pass AA. No blue or purple, including focus rings (use a 2 px black/white outline).

**Layout.** Homepage: the desk, and on it one page, about 1000 px wide on desktop: a text column of about 640 px and a right margin of about 300 px for balloons. Order: document title block ("Myles Parslow makes things with AI.", "Draft for review · 87 projects in 5 articles", review status line computed from the data), the lede and statistics as Recitals, the three routes as a numbered "Summary of terms", then the full index as Articles 1–5 with every project as a numbered section line (code, title, subtitle, tier, author bar), cross-references as "Related sections", rules as "General provisions", the tool schedule as a "Schedule 1" table, the archive notice as a footnote, and the footer as a signature block. Room pages: the same page format, the room as an Article, every case a Section with its full body restyled as document content (figures as "Figure 1.1-a" with captions), "How it was built" moved into the margin as that section's comment balloon (the text also stays in the body for no-CSS and parity), the "ask" box as a comment from Myles at the end.

**Motion.** None on scroll. The markup switch and filter change instantly; balloon leaders are drawn with CSS/SVG when a section is hovered or focused. One curve, 160 ms, for opacity only.

**Chrome.** A fixed review bar at the top of the window, outside the page: "Myles Parslow" (home), the view switch (All markup / No markup), the reviewer filter, the five Articles as links with counts, the theme switch. The Style dropdown will be inserted before the theme switch. On phones the review bar collapses to the view switch plus a "Review" menu; balloons drop inline under their section.

**A11y.** The markup state is real text with `aria-pressed` buttons; colour is never the only signal (each attributed sentence also carries visually hidden text "Attributed to Claude" etc., and a small author initial shows in the margin); balloons are `aside` elements linked with `aria-describedby`. No JS: all markup visible, all links work.

**Sameness check.** vs Working Drawings: type (serif document vs lettering sans), weight, layout (document + margin vs sheet + drawing list), imagery (none on the homepage vs line drawings), chrome (review bar vs title strip), motion (switch only vs plot wipes and fluid), signature all differ. vs Case File (court caption and manila folders, Caslon, exhibit stickers): ground, type, layout, chrome, motion and signature differ; do not use pleading line numbers, exhibit stickers or manila. vs Plain Web: type, layout, chrome, signature differ.
