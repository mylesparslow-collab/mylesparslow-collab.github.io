1A lawyer searches or asks a question

Example: "Have we acted for anyone in the Meridian Solar corporate family, and what indemnity caps did we agree?" Asked from Conflion, a firm tool, or a chat window.

2Who is asking? Check walls, confidentiality and role

One access rule decides what this person may see before anything is searched. Conflion already cuts each person's view of the data this way.

Wall stop 1

Walled matters are never searched, scored or counted. A hidden hit looks exactly like no hit, so nobody can probe for it.

3Read the matter wiki first

Each matter, client and attorney has a short wiki page: who is on it, the parties and their roles, key documents, open issues and decisions, and links to where everything else lives. The AI and the search read it first, so they know what to look for and where.

6The AI router decides, task by task, where this may go

Conflion's aiRoute.ts runs these checks in order on every AI call today. It has two routes now: local and Claude.

  • Can this person see every item referenced? If not, refuse.
  • Do the client's outside counsel guidelines bar AI? Refuse, or local only.
  • Is this task cleared for outside AI? If not, local only.
  • Scan the full outgoing text for walled matter, client and wall names. Any hit, local only.
Wall stop 3

Text that names a walled matter or party never goes to an outside AI. Widening the scan to adverse parties, aliases and former names is new work.

Three optional paths
Option A

A local model answers the whole thing

A model on firm hardware reads the results and writes the answer. Best for walled matters, sensitive clients, and anything a client's guidelines keep off outside AI.

Where we have a choice

  • Whether to stop at a list of results or ask for an AI answer.
  • Which of the three paths each kind of task takes. The router can default by task and still override per matter.
  • Which outside model Option B and Option C use. Claude today; Copilot and other APIs could be added.
  • How strict the lawyer check in Option B is, and who signs off.
  • Who owns each matter wiki page and approves changes to it.

What is real today

  • Conflion, a standalone prototype on fictional data, already enforces walls, searches by full text, and routes each AI task to local or Claude.
  • The matter wiki, anonymizing, meaning search, the Copilot and other-API routes, and live connections to Aderant and iManage are new work.
  • simbaOS is a written design for the shared layer, built from Conflion's pieces. It works alongside Aderant and iManage; it doesn't replace them.
