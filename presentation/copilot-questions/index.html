Copilot questions

Questions to ask Microsoft 365 Copilot at work before a software pilot, to learn how the firm’s computers, sign-in and policies are set up.

Keep client names out of what you ask it. None of these questions needs a client, a matter or a person’s name.

Sign-in

  1. 1What Microsoft 365 tenant and sign-in do we use? Is it Entra ID, and is multi-factor required?

This computer

  1. 2What version of Windows is this PC on, and is it 64-bit?
  2. 3Am I allowed to install apps on this computer, or do I need admin rights?
  3. 4Do we use Intune or the Company Portal for installing software?
  4. 5What antivirus or endpoint security does the firm use?

Firm policy

  1. 6Is there a firm policy on installing new software or running pilots? How do I request a pilot of new software, and who signs off on it?
  2. 7Is there a firm policy on using AI tools with client data?

Systems and people

  1. 8Who in IT handles app approvals or app registrations in Entra ID?
  2. 9Do we have servers or virtual machines, in the office or in Azure, and who manages them?
  3. 10Which version of Aderant do we use (Expert or Expert Sierra)?
  4. 11Who usually runs Aderant reports or exports here?
Copilot only knows what the firm’s Microsoft 365 holds, so check anything important with IT.
