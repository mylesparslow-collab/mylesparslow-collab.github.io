How I use AI

"discuss how Myles is using AI"

  • Every day, as a build partner. I design the tool and the rules, AI writes most of the code, and I test it.
  • I follow what Anthropic and others release and try it the same week. That's the knowledge gap I can fill.
  • I give the AI context before it answers: a running wiki it reads first. It's the same idea as a map of each matter. Don't show the real doc on screen.

Likely questions, short answers

"Claude security is our roadblock. Why move now?" Agreed, it's the right gate. Start with fictional data, keep sensitive work on local AI or anonymize it first, block walled names, and let IT and Marty set the rules. I can help test against them.

"Why not just buy Harvey or Legora?" They might fit some needs, and that's leadership's call. They sell the general layer. What's specific to us, like our walls, our clients' billing guidelines and our SNF work, still has to be built or tailored.

"Doesn't Claude or Copilot already do this?" Partly. Claude's New client intake template does a basic name check, much like Conflion's front door. My builds cover what's specific to us.

"Is client data safe?" Every demo uses fictional data. Anything loaded into Claude's templates goes to Anthropic, so sensitive work goes through local AI or anonymization. Firm Facts needs sign-on before real data.

"Will this replace staff?" No. The goal is freeing time for higher-value work, and a lawyer reviews every output.

"How will AI change our pricing?" That's leadership's call. I can help measure what the tools actually save, so any pricing decision rests on real numbers.

"Can we use these tomorrow?" They're ready to demo, not cleared for use. Each is a standalone prototype, and connecting them is the first build.

"So it wins 54% of cases?" No. 14 of 26 is a look back at past cases, not a win rate.

"How much money does this save?" No ROI claims. Hour Catch ran on one invented day, so never annualize it.

"Does this replace Aderant, iManage, Intapp or our conflicts system?" No. The tools work alongside them, and Conflion has been tested at about 200 lawyers. I don't claim the name-match test beats Intapp or Aderant.

"Is that search using real client names?" No. Meridian Solar and every party in it is fictional.

"Was Copilot Kit tested in our Microsoft 365?" No. Its tests ran on Claude Haiku, not inside the firm's tenant.

"Who owns the code?" That needs a lawyer's view before anything is handed over, especially Conflion.

"How fast can you build?" Very fast. Conflion alone has 300+ merged changes. Give no timelines or dates.
