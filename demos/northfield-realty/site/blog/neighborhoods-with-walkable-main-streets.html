Neighborhoods with walkable main streets

Quick answer

Elm Hollow, Stonebridge and Highview each have a compact center with shops and cafes a short stroll from many homes. Their charm differs: a village green, a gallery corridor and a plaza.

Why this matters

Walkable centers reduce short car trips and make errands easy. Visit on a weekday evening and a weekend morning to see how the streets feel.

Elm Hollow

Cafes, a bookshop and a bakery cluster around Hollow Green. Read the Elm Hollow guide for more.

Stonebridge

Foundry St has galleries, a ceramics studio and a taproom. Read the Stonebridge guide for more.

Highview

Orchard Ln and the plaza hold restaurants and a food-truck night. Read the Highview guide for more.

Ashford Park

Family-run restaurants and a library sit within a few blocks of the park. Read the Ashford Park guide for more.
