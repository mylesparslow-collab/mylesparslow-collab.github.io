Selling an inherited home: a step-by-step guide

NR
Northfield Realty teamSample article · 6 min read ·
Quick answer

Selling an inherited home begins with confirming who has legal authority to sell, usually through the estate process. Then decide whether to repair, clean out and list as-is, get a valuation and work with an agent who has handled estates before.

In this article
  1. Confirm authority
  2. Establish value
  3. Prepare the property
  4. List and sell
  5. Frequently asked questions

Confirm authority

The personal representative or executor typically signs. A probate attorney can explain the documents required by your local court.

Establish value

An appraisal or agent analysis documents the value at the date of inheritance, which can matter for taxes. Ask a tax professional.

Prepare the property

Sort belongings, secure valuables and decide what to repair. Some sellers list as-is. See our camera-ready checklist and pre-sale inspection.

List and sell

Disclosure duties still apply; read disclosure vs. disclaimer. Take your time with the decisions. Our team is happy to work at your pace.

Frequently asked questions

Can heirs disagree?

Yes. Mediation and legal guidance can help.

How long does it take?

Timelines vary by court and estate complexity.
