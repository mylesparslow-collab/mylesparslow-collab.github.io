Larkfield vs. Willow Bend: comparing two neighborhoods

NR
Northfield Realty teamSample article · 5 min read ·
Quick answer

Larkfield offers newer construction, wide lawns and community amenities, while Willow Bend offers bungalows beside a protected meadow preserve. Both have their own character; the right choice depends on the kind of home, outdoor space and daily routine you want.

  3. Getting around
  4. What to check before buying

Homes and lots

Larkfield: Two-story colonials, transitional designs and newer craftsman-style homes are typical, often with attached garages, finished basements and generous yards. Newer sections have energy-efficient builder specs.

Willow Bend: Bungalows, cottages and cape cods dominate, with deep front porches, garden beds and detached garages. Several lots have been renovated with efficient additions, and a handful of new builds echo the older scale.

Things to do

Larkfield: A community pool, tennis courts, a nature path around the meadows and a seasonal fireworks night are anchored at the community center. A shopping plaza with groceries and services is minutes away.

Willow Bend: The preserve trail is a favorite spot for birdwatching, and a community garden, a pottery co-op and a Sunday farm stand give the neighborhood a relaxed rhythm. A small trailhead opens onto the greenway.

Getting around

Larkfield: Larkfield is a drive-oriented neighborhood with easy access to the ring road and an express bus to downtown at peak hours.

Willow Bend: Willow Bend is a short drive from downtown and has a bus line along its main road. A greenway trail connects to Brookside.

What to check before buying

Larkfield: Ask whether the neighborhood has an association, what the dues cover and what the rules are for fences, sheds and exterior colors. In newer sections, ask about builder warranties and the age of mechanical systems.

Willow Bend: Ask about drainage history and grading, and request an updated survey. Check for moisture in crawlspaces and foundations, and for the age of septic or sewer connections. Garden-friendly lots are common, so ask about drainage.

How to decide

Tour both on the same day, at the same time, and compare how each fits your routine. Use our checklist, then read each full guide: Larkfield and Willow Bend.

Frequently asked questions

Which has larger lots, Larkfield or Willow Bend?

Larkfield features larger yards; Willow Bend features cottage lots. Your agent can show examples of each.

Can I tour both in one day?

Yes. Our agents regularly plan back-to-back neighborhood tours.
