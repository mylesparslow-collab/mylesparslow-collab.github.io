The camera-ready home: a room-by-room prep checklist

NR
Northfield Realty teamSample article · 6 min read ·
Quick answer

Buyers see your home online first, so presentation begins with the photos. Declutter, clean, brighten and stage each room. A few inexpensive updates, such as fresh paint, bulbs and tidy landscaping, often make the biggest impact.

In this article
  1. Entry and exterior
  2. Living areas
  3. Kitchen and dining
  4. Bedrooms and baths
  5. Photo-day routine
  6. Frequently asked questions

Entry and exterior

Power-wash walkways, trim shrubs and refresh the front door. Make sure house numbers and lighting work. First impressions start at the curb.

Living areas

Remove extra furniture, personal collections and visible cords. Open blinds and use warm, consistent bulbs. Arrange seating to show how the room can be used.

Kitchen and dining

Clear counters except for one or two simple items. Clean the stove, sink and cabinet fronts. Replace worn hardware if it is inexpensive.

Bedrooms and baths

Simple bedding, empty closets by a third and sparkling fixtures make rooms feel larger. Replace worn towels with fresh, neutral ones.

Photo-day routine

  • Turn on every light
  • Hide trash cans and pet items
  • Move cars off the driveway
  • Leave during the shoot

Frequently asked questions

Do I need to stage?

Light staging, even just rearranging what you own, helps. Your agent can advise.

How long does prep take?

Most homes take one to three weeks.
