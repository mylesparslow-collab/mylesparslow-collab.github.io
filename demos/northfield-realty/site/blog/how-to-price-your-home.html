How to price your home to sell

NR
Northfield Realty teamSample article · 5 min read ·
Quick answer

Pricing starts with recent comparable sales, adjusts for condition, features and location, and then factors in current demand. A home priced in line with the market draws more showings early, when attention is highest.

In this article
  1. Start with comparables
  2. Adjust for differences
  3. Choose a strategy
  4. Review early feedback
  5. Frequently asked questions

Start with comparables

Look at similar homes that sold in the past few months nearby, matching size, age, style and condition. Active and pending listings show your competition.

Adjust for differences

Updates, lot, parking and layout all matter. Your agent adjusts the comparables to estimate what a buyer would pay for your home.

Choose a strategy

A price slightly below market can spark competing offers; a price above may reduce showings. Discuss risk tolerance and your timeline.

Review early feedback

If showings are few or feedback repeats, revisit price and presentation. See our camera-ready checklist and pre-sale inspections.

Get listing alerts

Tell us where you are looking and we will send new matches. This is a demo; nothing is sent.

Frequently asked questions

Does an online estimate match an agent analysis?

Online estimates are a starting point. A local agent accounts for condition and neighborhood nuances.

When should I reduce the price?

If there is little interest after the first couple of weeks, discuss options.

Sources

  • Your local assessor, recorder or municipal office (confirm current rules)
  • A licensed attorney, tax professional or lender for your situation
  • The local MLS and your Northfield Realty agent for current market conditions
