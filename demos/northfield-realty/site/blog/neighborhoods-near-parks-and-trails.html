Neighborhoods near parks and trails

NR
Northfield Realty teamSample article · 5 min read ·
Quick answer

Several of our neighborhoods are organized around parks, trails or preserves: Ashford Park around its central green, Brookside along its greenway trail, Cedar Ridge beside a hilltop preserve and Willow Bend next to a meadow trail.

Why this matters

If time outdoors is part of your routine, the setting around a home matters as much as the house itself. Visit at different times of day and in different weather.

Ashford Park

The central park, garden pavilion, promenade and ball fields frame daily life here. Read the Ashford Park guide for more.

Brookside

The greenway trail runs the length of the valley with overlooks and playgrounds. Read the Brookside guide for more.

Cedar Ridge

The Ridge Preserve offers hiking paths and a sunrise overlook. Read the Cedar Ridge guide for more.

Willow Bend

A walking trail through a protected meadow is a favorite spot for birdwatching. Read the Willow Bend guide for more.

Frequently asked questions

Do trails raise or lower values?

Proximity to open space is often valued, but conditions vary. Ask your agent for local comparables.

Are there drainage considerations near trail corridors?

Check the grade and drainage, and request a thorough inspection of lower levels.
