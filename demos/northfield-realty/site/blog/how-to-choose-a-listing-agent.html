How to choose the right listing agent: 10 questions to ask

NR
Northfield Realty teamSample article · 6 min read ·
Quick answer

The best listing agent for you explains the plan clearly, shows recent comparable results, communicates on a schedule you are comfortable with and puts fees and terms in writing. Interview at least two agents before deciding.

In this article
  1. Experience and approach
  2. Marketing plan
  3. Communication
  4. Terms and fees
  5. Trust your read
  6. Frequently asked questions

Experience and approach

  • How long have you worked in this area?
  • How many homes like mine have you sold recently?
  • What is your pricing approach?

Marketing plan

  • What photography and online exposure do you provide?
  • How will you attract buyer agents?
  • How do you handle showings and feedback?

Communication

  • How often will you update me?
  • Who is my day-to-day contact?
  • How do you handle offers?

Terms and fees

  • What are your fees and what do they cover?
  • How long is the listing agreement?
  • What happens if we want to change course?

Trust your read

Beyond credentials, notice whether the agent listens and explains. Meet our team in a pricing consultation and compare notes.

Get listing alerts

Tell us where you are looking and we will send new matches. This is a demo; nothing is sent.

Frequently asked questions

Should I choose the highest estimate?

Not necessarily. Compare the evidence behind each recommendation.

Can I cancel an agreement?

Terms vary. Ask before signing.
