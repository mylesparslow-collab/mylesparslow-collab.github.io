How to choose a neighborhood: a checklist

NR
Northfield Realty teamSample article · 5 min read ·
Quick answer

Compare neighborhoods using the same checklist: the homes, lots and amenities you want, your commute, ongoing costs and how the place feels at different times of day. Tour in person at least twice.

In this article
  1. The homes
  2. Amenities and green space
  3. Getting around
  4. Ongoing costs
  5. Visit more than once
  6. Frequently asked questions

The homes

Which architecture and layouts do you like? Do you want a yard, a porch or low-maintenance living?

Amenities and green space

Where are the parks, trails, shops and services you will use weekly?

Getting around

Test the commute at your hours and consider transit and parking.

Ongoing costs

Compare taxes, dues and insurance. See property taxes.

Visit more than once

Go on a weekday evening and a weekend morning. Use our weekend tour as a template. Then read the individual guides, such as Elm Hollow or Larkfield.

Get listing alerts

Tell us where you are looking and we will send new matches. This is a demo; nothing is sent.

Frequently asked questions

How many neighborhoods should I compare?

Three or four is a manageable shortlist.

Should I rely on rankings?

Use factual information about homes and amenities, and decide with your own priorities.

Sources

  • Your local assessor, recorder or municipal office (confirm current rules)
  • A licensed attorney, tax professional or lender for your situation
  • The local MLS and your Northfield Realty agent for current market conditions
