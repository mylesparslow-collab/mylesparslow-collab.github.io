Neighborhoods with larger lots

NR
Northfield Realty teamSample article · 5 min read ·
Quick answer

Cedar Ridge and Larkfield offer the largest lots, from an acre-plus wooded sites to wide suburban lawns. Willow Bend and Elm Hollow offer garden-scale and mature lots.

Why this matters

Larger lots offer privacy and space but also upkeep. Ask about drainage, tree rules and private septic or well systems where applicable.

Cedar Ridge

Lots of an acre or more with walk-out lower levels and long driveways. Read the Cedar Ridge guide for more.

Larkfield

Wide lawns, finished basements and a community pool and meadows. Read the Larkfield guide for more.

Willow Bend

Garden-friendly cottage lots beside a meadow preserve. Read the Willow Bend guide for more.

Elm Hollow

Mature, tree-shaded lots around the green. Read the Elm Hollow guide for more.

Frequently asked questions

Do larger lots need septic?

Some do. Ask your agent.

Is there an association?

Larkfield has one; ask for dues and rules.
