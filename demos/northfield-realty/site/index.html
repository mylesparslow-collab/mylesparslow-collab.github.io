Questions

Frequently asked questions

Who is Northfield Realty?

Northfield Realty is a fictional, independent boutique real estate brokerage in a mid-size city. It exists as a portfolio demo, and every name, listing and figure on this page is sample data.

What areas does Northfield serve?

The firm represents buyers and sellers across the city and nearby suburbs, including Elm Hollow, Brookside, Cedar Ridge, Stonebridge, Highview, Ashford Park, Larkfield and Willow Bend.

How does the selling process work?

An agent prepares a pricing analysis from recent comparable sales, plans photography and staging, lists the home on the local MLS and manages showings, offers and the path to closing. Compensation is discussed and agreed with you up front.

How do I find out what my home is worth?

Request a free home valuation using the form on this page. An agent prepares a personal pricing analysis based on recent comparable sales near your home, usually within 24 hours.

Do you work with first-time buyers?

Yes. The team walks first-time buyers through financing, inspections, offers and closing in plain language, and helps set a realistic budget before the search begins.
