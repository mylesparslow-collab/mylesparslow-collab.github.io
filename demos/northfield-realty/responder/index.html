    Guardrails
    
  
    Fast, and always inside the lines
    
  
    Speed is only useful if every reply is one Dana would be happy to put her name next to. The responder follows a fixed playbook; the language model only fills in the words.
    
  
    
    
    It will always
      
      
    • Identify itself as Northfield's assistant and promise a real agent will follow up.
      • 
      
    • Answer an inbound inquiry at any hour, then hold outbound follow-ups for 8 am to 9 pm.
      • 
      
    • Text only a number the visitor gave with consent, and honor STOP instantly.
      • 
      
    • Hand off the moment someone asks for a person, with the on-call agent paged by text.
      • 
      
    • Keep facts to what is in the listing, the site, and public sources, and say so when it does not know.
      • 
      
    • Log every message to the CRM so the agent reads the whole thread before calling.
      • 
    
    
    
    It will never
      
      
    • Ask about or comment on race, color, religion, national origin, sex, familial status, disability, or any other protected class, including locally added ones (marital status, sexual orientation, gender identity, source of income).
      • 
      
    • Describe who lives in a neighborhood, call an area "safe", "family-friendly" or "up-and-coming", or rate schools. It points to objective public data instead.
      • 
      
    • Steer a buyer toward or away from an area. It searches by the criteria the buyer gives.
      • 
      
    • Quote a value, a rate, or a legal or tax conclusion. Those come from the agent, the lender, or the title company.
      • 
      
    • Pretend to be a person, or keep texting someone who has opted out.
      •

    Why the first minutes matter. The lead-response research that agencies have cited since the 2011 Harvard Business Review study ("The Short Life of Online Sales Leads") found that reaching a web lead within minutes makes a qualified conversation far more likely than reaching them an hour later. Listing portals also rank agents partly on response speed. Most brokerages still answer the next morning.

    The website's forms and calculators already capture, score and route every lead. The responder listens to that same stream, so nothing on the site changes.
