Post-closing review engine

Every closing becomes a public five-star review, without anyone remembering to ask.

Northfield Realty already earns strong ratings. The gap is volume and visibility: only a handful of public reviews compared with the homes the team has sold. This system asks every client at the right moment, in the agent's own voice, and sends the review where it does the most good.

Behind the page

Tap a rating to see the routingThe page is personal to the client and the agent: the link in every text and email is unique, so the engine knows who opened it and when.
Why Google first. Google reviews are the biggest single factor in who appears in the Maps results for "realtor near me". Agent-directory reviews matter when a buyer or seller is already shopping agents there, so they are the second destination, pointed at the agent's own profile.
Policy note. Google prohibits "review gating" (only inviting happy clients to post). This design asks everyone and shows everyone the public links. The rating step exists so an unhappy client reaches Dana immediately, not so the ask disappears.

5 · Going live

What it takes to switch this on.

Nothing here depends on a new CRM. The engine needs three things from the brokerage and about two weeks of setup.

From the brokerage

Three inputs

  • The Google review link from the Business Profile dashboard (a short link).
  • Each agent's directory review-request link, so reviews land on the right profile and on the team total.
  • A closing trigger. Simplest is a two-field form the agent fills at settlement; better is a feed from whatever transaction tool the office uses. Which tool that is depends on the office.

Plumbing

How messages go out

  • Texts through a registered business texting number per agent, with the agent's name and an opt-out line. Clients are existing customers, so the outreach is permitted, and STOP is honored instantly.
  • Emails from the agent's own firm email address, with proper authentication so they reach the inbox.
  • Approval by a one-tap text to the agent before each send, which can be switched off per agent once they trust the drafts.

Pilot

How success is measured

  • Reviews per month on Google and the second site, before and after.
  • Ask-to-review rate, by agent and by touch, so the messages can be tuned.
  • Maps visibility for "realtor near me" in the firm's core neighborhoods.
  • Private feedback caught before it became a public review.
