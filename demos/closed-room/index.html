    Why on-device matters

    Privilege can be lost when a confidential conversation is disclosed to a third party. Here no third party receives it: the speech model runs in your browser, and the page's security policy blocks uploads to any outside server.

    What it can't fix

    A transcript is protected only if the conversation itself was privileged. A verbatim record can be ordered produced if privilege is challenged or waived. Keep only what you need, and follow your firm's retention and litigation-hold rules.

    Keep it local

    Exported files go wherever you save them. A folder synced to iCloud, OneDrive or Google Drive puts them back in the cloud. This is general information, not legal advice.
