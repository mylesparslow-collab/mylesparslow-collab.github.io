Bead-strand simulator assets: credits (all CC0 / public-domain-equivalent)

Human figures (child_hand.glb, child_talk.glb, child_star.glb)
  MPFB2 (MakeHuman Plugin for Blender) v2.0.18 base mesh, rig and the MakeHuman system asset pack
  (hair, shoes and eyes meshes are from this pack). The MakeHuman base meshes and system assets are CC0 1.0.
  https://static.makehumancommunity.org/mpfb.html
  Clothing (shirt/top, shorts/trousers, socks) was generated in Blender as offset shells of the body mesh. Skin, hair,
  clothing and shoe materials are flat colours made by us. Eye texture: MPFB system pack brown_eye.png (CC0).

Wood textures (props.glb: chair, desk)
  "Oak Veneer 01" by Jenelle van Heerden, via Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com/a/oak_veneer_01), CC0 1.0.
  Diffuse, GL normal and roughness maps at 1k resolution (1024 px), JPG.

Chair and desk geometry modelled by us in Blender (blender/build_props.py), referencing the layout of the
school desk and chair in the reference photo supplied with the task.
