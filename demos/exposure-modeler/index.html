How the outcomes spread 10,000 simulated runs of the tree

Each bar counts how many simulated runs ended at that net result after fees. Dark bars sit between the 10th and 90th percentile. The green line marks the average result after fees. The settlement figure that beats that average is the break-even in the answer above.

What moves the answer each stage's key chance, plus or minus 10 points

Change in net expected value if that chance goes down or up by 10 points (the other branches rebalance). Longest bar matters most.
