# Hollowfell store: jury sheet (/websiteoftheyear)

Tier **B** (DOM-first, SVG signature, no GL). Primary pattern: **Opal Tadpole scroll-pinned product teardown** (one object, pinned, taken apart by the scroll). Secondary: **Teenage Engineering catalogue rows** (a spec table you can buy from, no card grid). Borrowed details: Aesop's quiet side-drawer product page, Mammut Eiger Extreme's field-data readouts, Obys-style typed page labels.

- **Concept:** a fire lookout's logbook that sells knives. Every tool has an accession number like a museum specimen, a typed spec row, and a field log. The site is lichen-grey ledger paper with one margin rule and one stamp colour. The buyer is the keeper of a record, not the owner of a weapon. What it should feel like: picking up a tool your grandfather oiled. What good it does: repair, self-reliance, one object kept for 30 years.
- **Default named, convention broken:** the default knife store is black, tactical, camo, shouting caps, glossy product-on-black grid. Broken on purpose: **no black hero and no product photo grid; the products are ink line drawings on paper.** Reason: a thing you keep for thirty years belongs in a register, and a drawing shows how it works where a photo only shows that it is shiny.
- **Odd reference:** a US Forest Service fire-lookout logbook (date, place, wind, remarks, written daily whether or not anything burned), crossed with a museum specimen label. What it changed: products got accession numbers; the cart became a requisition slip; the item count is tally marks; the testing story is a five-line log that ends in a pencilled "18 minutes. told you."; checkout is a rubber stamp.
- **Signature interaction:** *Open it slowly.* A cutaway slipjoint pinned for 430vh. Scroll swings the blade 0 to 180 degrees round its pivot along a protractor arc. It **parks at the 90 degree half-stop** for a stretch of scroll, as a real slipjoint does (the one beat of tension), the backspring visibly bows between stops and rests at 0, 90 and 180, the figure ticks when it seats, and a readout counts degrees and names the state (Closed, Lifting, Half-stop, Spring loading, Open). A range lever does the same by hand and by keyboard.
- **Loader → hero:** no loader. The headline rises out of a mask (1s) while the hero knife draws its own outline (stroke-dashoffset, 1.6s) and the specimen label drops on with a 1.4 degree tilt. All CSS, all from a visible end state.
- **Type:** Archivo variable (width 62 to 75 for display, 100 for body; weight 400/600/700) + Courier Prime for typed labels and the log + Caveat for two pencil notes only. Display 3rem to 8.8rem, line-height .88. Sentence case throughout; no shouting caps on headlines.
- **Palette:** paper #e6e9e1, shade #d8dcd1, leaf #f3f5ef, ink #16201c, pencil #566159, rule #aeb6a8, stamp indigo #2a3ec0 (the only accent), pine #12201b (one dark page, for steel). CSS custom properties. Single theme by choice.
- **Motion:** vanilla JS, no library (rAF-throttled scroll, about 5 KB). Curve cubic-bezier(.19,1,.22,1) out; stamp uses (.2,1.4,.4,1) for the overshoot. Durations .22s (tick) to 1.6s (hero draw). Blade travel eased in-out cubic per segment so it leaves and seats softly.
- **Chrome:** sticky paper header with a miniature knife in the wordmark whose blade opens as you scroll the page (closed at the top, open at the footer). Slip tab top right with tally marks. One indigo margin rule down the left edge.
- **Delight:** the half-stop; tally marks; "Strike off" instead of "Remove"; the DEMO stamp landing with a thud; the twelfth tool nobody could explain; a blade that smells of apples.
- **Commerce:** 8 products. Client-side requisition slip (add, quantity, strike off, subtotal, postage rule), kept in localStorage when available. Checkout is a stamp: "DEMO. No payment taken." No card fields anywhere. Demo notice in the top strip, the slip, the FAQ and the footer.
- **Sound:** none.
- **Mobile:** rows restack as specimen cards; 44px minimum tap targets; sheets go full width; the fold shows one station at a time under the knife; steel table and log restack with inline labels. No horizontal scroll at 390.
- **Budget:** 76 KB of HTML including all drawings and script, three font families, no script libraries. Photos are lazy-loaded and optional: a frame that fails to load removes itself.
- **A11y:** real buttons and links, native `<dialog>` sheets (focus trap, Esc), `<details>` FAQ, live region for slip changes, visible focus ring, range input for the signature. Reduced motion or no JS gives a static, fully visible page with the knife open and every station readable.
- **Calls made without asking:** brand name Hollowfell (checked: no knife maker by that name turned up); Pexels over Unsplash (IDs sit in the page URL, so they verify cleanly); vanilla JS over GSAP/Lenis (smaller, and testable in a sandbox that blocks CDNs); steels named by grade only.
