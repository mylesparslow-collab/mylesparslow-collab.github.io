Open it slowly.

This is the Tarn. No lock, one spring, 52 grams. Scroll and the blade follows your hand. Collectors call the pull and the snap of a slipjoint its walk and talk. This is the walk.

180°Open

  1. 000°

    Closed. The edge sits inside, against the spring. In a pocket it is a smooth 9 cm bar. Your keys can't reach the blade.

  2. 030°

    Nail nick. A thumbnail in the nick lifts the blade. The steel pushes back. That push is what keeps it shut in your pocket.

  3. 090°

    Half-stop. The tang has a flat here, so the blade parks at ninety degrees. If your grip slips while closing, it stops on the flat and not on your fingers.

  4. 135°

    Spring loading. The backspring bows 0.6 mm at its peak. Ours did this 20,000 times on the test rig and hasn't taken a set.

  5. 180°

    Open. The spring drops onto the tang and the spine sits flush with the handle. Run a thumb along the back. There is no step.

it stops at 90 on purpose. keep going.

Four steels. Each one gives something up.

No steel wins every column. These are our shop ratings out of five, from sharpening and breaking our own samples.

D2Lookout4232Keeps biting long after it stops shaving. Wipe it dry and it will forgive you.
80CrV2Drover, Ember striker3154Carbon steel. Rusts if you ignore it, goes grey and handsome if you don't.

Three degrees decide what the knife is for.

We grind the folders to 17° a side. That edge slides through a tomato skin under its own weight. The Drover gets 20°, which is thicker behind the edge and survives a knot in oak. The brass guide in the Whet kit holds both angles, so you don't have to guess.

  1. Care, rule 1Dry it.

    Water doesn't hurt steel. Water left overnight does. Wipe the blade on your trouser leg before it goes back in the pocket.

  2. Care, rule 2One drop.

    When the pivot squeaks, give it one drop of oil. Any oil. Open and close it ten times and wipe off what comes out.

  3. Care, rule 3Sharpen early.

    Touch it up when it stops slicing paper cleanly. A dull knife takes an hour to fix and a tired one takes five minutes.

Page 6 · Questions

Asked at the counter.

Short answers. The first one matters most.

Is this a real shop?

No. Hollowfell is a fictional maker and this is a demo store. You can fill a requisition slip and stamp it, but no payment is taken, no card is asked for and nothing ships.

How would shipping work?

Tools would leave the workshop within two working days by tracked post: a flat $9, free over $150. An adult signs for anything with an edge.

What does the warranty cover?

Thirty years on materials and workmanship. It follows the knife and not the buyer, so it still counts when you hand the knife down. We sharpen any Hollowfell for free, for life; you pay the postage. Prying, throwing and losing it are on you.

Can I carry it where I live?

That depends on where you live. Rules on blade length, locking blades and carrying in public differ between countries, states and cities, and they change. Check your local law before you carry any knife, and never take one through airport security. We can't give legal advice. Sales are to adults only.

What if I don't get on with it?

Send it back unused within 60 days for a full refund. If you have used it, send it back anyway and tell us what was wrong. We would rather know.
