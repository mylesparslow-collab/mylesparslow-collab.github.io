Tool 02

Tip, or service charge?

Box 12, code TP

A tip is voluntary: the customer sets the amount and can leave nothing. Anything mandatory is a service charge — wages, not tips — even when every cent goes to staff. Pick what happened at the till.

Eligibility follows the occupation performed when the tip was received, not the employer’s industry. An occupation absent from this table does not qualify, however customary tipping is in that job. Amounts a manager or supervisor takes out of a tip pool are not qualified tips.

Scope

What this page will not do

It does not calculate anyone’s deduction. That is settled on the employee’s own return, against caps and income limits.

It does not decide whether an occupation qualifies. It reproduces the table so you can read it yourself.

It is not tax, legal or payroll advice. Confirm the current Form W‑2 instructions before filing.

Sources. Occupation table: 26 CFR 1.224‑1(h), table 1, final rule published 13 April 2026, effective 12 June 2026, applying to tax years 2025–2028. Premium rule: Fair Labor Standards Act §7 and IRS guidance on the qualified overtime deduction. Checked 8 September 2026.
