After your visit

Clear Aftercare

Paste the instructions from a clinic, ER or hospital visit. You get a plain-language care sheet and one-click calendar reminders for every dose, task and appointment.

1. Paste the instructions

Type or paste exactly what the doctor, nurse or discharge paper says. The tool only rewrites what is there. It never adds medicines, doses or times. Anything unclear is marked for you to check.

Making a new sheet asks Claude to read the text, so you may be asked to allow that the first time.

2. Put it all in your calendar

One file with every reminder below. It opens in Apple Calendar, Outlook and most phones. For Google Calendar, use the Google buttons on each item.
