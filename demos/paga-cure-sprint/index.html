PAGA Cure Sprint

Penalty exposure and cure deadlines under California's reformed Private Attorneys General Act (2024 amendments), for a fictional employer that has just received a notice (60 sampled employees, about a year of pay periods). Change an assumption on the left and every figure recalculates. The same rules drive a downloadable Excel model where every number is a live formula.
