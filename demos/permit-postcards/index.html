      About the data

      Every dot is a real building permit from a city's public records. When someone pulls a permit for a kitchen, a pool or a new home, the trades that come after it (countertops, fencing, landscaping and so on) are the ones who get a postcard.

      Loading permit data…

      Each dot that matches your trade is a lead. Set its status, add a follow-up date and notes, and they stay with it. Leads are ranked Hot, Warm or Cool from how well the timing fits your trade, the project size and the neighborhood (public Census numbers), compared with the other leads in your area. Statuses and notes are shared with everyone who can open this page; if you opened a local copy, they are saved on this device only.
