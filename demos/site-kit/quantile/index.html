Questions

Will it fit my diffuser?

Yes if your diffuser is a cold air (also called nebulizing or waterless) unit that holds oil in a bottle you can refill. Unscrew your diffuser's bottle and pour ours in. No for ultrasonic diffusers that mix oil into water, reed diffusers, plug in warmers, or diffusers that only take sealed brand pods or vials.

How long does a bottle last?

It depends on your diffuser and its setting. Most homes running a diffuser in the evenings get about a month from 120 mL. If yours lasts longer, switch to every 2 months from any order email.

How does it work?

Pick a scent and a schedule: monthly for a diffuser that runs most evenings, every two months for lighter use. The bottle arrives in 2 to 5 days; fill your diffuser's oil bottle and keep the rest capped. We ship the next one before you run out. Reply to any order email to swap scents, skip a delivery or cancel.

Is this the same scent as a hotel I stayed at?

No. These are our own four blends, built in the same style as hotel lobby scents: bright on top, warm and musky underneath. We do not copy or name any hotel's signature scent.

Can I use it in an ultrasonic diffuser?

No. The oil is undiluted and made for cold air diffusers that nebulize oil directly. In a water diffuser it can damage the plate and will not spread properly.

How do I change scents or skip a month?

Reply to any order email with what you want: a different scent, a skipped delivery, a new schedule, or a cancellation. Changes made at least 3 days before your next delivery apply to that delivery.

What if I don't like my first scent?

Tell us within 30 days and we send a different scent at no charge. Keep or pass on the first bottle.

Is it safe around pets?

Fragrance oils can bother cats, birds and small animals. Run your diffuser where pets can leave the room, keep the setting low, and ask your vet if you are unsure.

Where do you ship?

Anywhere in the United States, free. Orders leave within 1 to 2 business days and arrive in 2 to 5 by ground mail. We do not ship outside the US yet.

Can I return a bottle?

Unopened bottles can come back within 30 days for a full refund. Email us with your order number and we send instructions.
