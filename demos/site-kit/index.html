Site Kit demo sites

Four sites built by Site Kit from a content file, with no AI at build time. The businesses and products are made up, and the checkouts are demos that charge nothing.

  • Example PlumbingTrades template: one page for a local service business.
  • Example StoreStore template: a shop of unique items with filters and a cart.
  • Example BottleProduct template: one hero product, sold like a direct-to-consumer brand.
  • Quantile Scent ClubProduct template: a concept of my own.
