Other items paid up front with the first payment (optional):

The rest of labor is split evenly across the phases. Materials and windows & doors are due with the first payment.

Wording and payments

Payment groups: leave empty for one payment per phase with 10% of labor up front and 10% at the end (the Whitcomb way). Or list which phases each payment covers, like 1-3; 4; 5-7: labor is split in equal shares, one with the first payment and one per group, with materials in the first. Add + dumpster to a group to bill the dump fee with it.

Phases

One step per line; numbering is automatic and runs through the whole job. To remove a phase, clear its name.
