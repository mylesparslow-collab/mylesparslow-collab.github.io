  Method note
  
Jurymech is a simulation, not a prediction. Each juror is written by the same language model, so the jury shares one model's blind spots and habits of mind. That makes it useful for finding which facts carry weight and what would move a verdict, and a poor tool for forecasting what a real jury would do.

  
    
    
  • Criminal cases need a unanimous verdict. Anything else is a hung jury.
    • 
    
  • Civil cases use the California civil rule, 3/4: 9 of 12 jurors, or 5 of 6 on a six-person jury.
    • 
    
  • Jurors may reason only from the facts you give them, and they may argue or misremember.
    • 
    
  • Up to four rounds: straw poll, discussion, a second discussion if the vote is not unanimous, then a final vote.
