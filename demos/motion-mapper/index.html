Motion Mapper

How a judge, or a law firm, has fared on five kinds of motion, read from docket entries. Fictional demo data.

All judges, firms, parties and cases in the demo are fictional. Every figure describes invented dockets only and says nothing about any real judge or firm. Code reads the docket and does all the counting; the model is used only to tag entries the rules could not read, and its tags are kept only when its quoted evidence appears word for word in the entry.
